Colors Balance
It’s important to maintain a balanced and harmonious
usage of iagon brand palette. Our purple gradient, for
example, should not be too predominant on any UI
interface, or on any poster / online graphic. 




Always favor light or dark backgrounds, and use our
purples as accent colors to drive attention to the
important bits of informations




White Space Balance
Keep the design breathable, with loads of white space
to enhance readability, and direct the users focus on
important things.
