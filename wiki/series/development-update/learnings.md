# Development Update: learnings

## From the October 2026 issue

**Multi-image posts in X need square cards.** Two 1200×700 cards were cropped from the centre to about 7:8; the logo and the series name were lost. Even a square is cropped by ~70 px per side, so side padding must be at least 112 px. The composer preview shows the crop: check there before posting.

**Weekly and monthly do different jobs.** Once the weekly update carries every change, a full list in the monthly is a repeat. The monthly shows scale and direction; the weekly is the record.

**Counts per area in the text hurt.** "Storage Node: 44" above four lines points at the 40 that are missing, and "Admin Dashboard: 12" above "Mesh SDK: 9" reads as a priority that isn't real. The total and the category card carry the scale.

**Counts are a process metric.** "176 changes shipped" measures logged work, not value. The word "shipped" keeps the frame on delivery. The value goes in the text: what a reader can now use.

**A thread beats one long post** for a recap split by area: every area tweet is its own entry point that the right audience can repost.

**Grouping by Category tells the team, grouping by Area tells the reader.** The dashboard groups by Category because that is how work is filed. Readers relate to areas. The cards use both: areas as chips, categories as the grid.

**Gradient text did not survive review.** At 28 px the gradient between two purples is not visible as a gradient; it only reads as uneven colour. Solid accent per theme instead.

**Purple on the total number competed with the eyebrow.** When the accent marks everything it marks nothing. Purple stays on the system (eyebrow, Total tile tint); numbers are neutral.
