# Series

One folder per content series:

- `series.md`: idea, goal, audience, format, narrative arc, rules.
- `posts/`: one file per post. Records the post's role in the arc, hook and angle, links to other posts, and results.
- `learnings.md`: what worked, what didn't, what we change.

The point is to keep the logic of each series, not just an archive of texts.
