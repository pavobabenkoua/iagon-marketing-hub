# Format: two-panel post

Visual system of the Würth × Iagon series. Set with post 1, kept in posts 2 and 3. Works for any "before / after" or "today / what it takes" argument.

## Layout

- Title + subtitle top left, Iagon logo top right ([iagon-horizontal-on-light.svg](../logos/iagon-horizontal-on-light.svg)).
- Two panels: light `#F6F6FB` on the left, dark `#13181E` on the right.
- Under each panel: a purple eyebrow + the name of the state.
- Eyebrow pairs: `Today` / `Where it is going`, `Today` / `What it takes`.

## Color

- Purple text: `#6F47FF` on the light panel, `#7C75FF` on the dark panel ([decision 0004](../../../../decisions/0004-design-system.md)). Posts 1–3 used `#7E67FF` on dark; new posts use `#7C75FF`.
- Purple only on the eyebrow and one accent.

## Numbers on graphics

Any graphic with numbers or proportions carries a disclaimer: `Figures are illustrative. The point is the ratio, not the numbers.` or `Proportions are illustrative.` Bottom left, small, `#62687E`. Best: build the graphic without numbers at all, then no disclaimer is needed.

The original series doc set the disclaimer in `#9EA3B8`. That fails contrast on light (below 3:1), so it is replaced with Body `#62687E`.

## Bans (this format)

- No dashed frames, no neon, no hexagon grids, no mascot.

## Check every render

- Dashed guides from the mockup stay in the export.
- Panels come out with different visual weight (one side brighter or more styled).
- The element carrying the argument is smaller than the labels around it.
- The purple accent duplicates the logo color and reads as branding, not meaning.
