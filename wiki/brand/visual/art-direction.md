# Art direction

What Iagon visuals show and avoid. Comes from the [Brand Archetype](../strategy/archetype.md): Sage visuals signal credibility and depth, Caregiver visuals signal humanity and warmth. Iagon's visuals carry both.

Adapted from the "Visual Implications" block of the Brandbook Package and aligned with the approved design system (2026-11-12): typography follows [Colors and typography](colors-typography.md), the palette description follows the actual brand palette.

## Lead with

- **Real people in professional environments.** Doctors, engineers, compliance officers, bankers, in context, at work.
- **Architectural and system diagrams.** Clean, accurate, technical. A visible proof of rigor.
- **Document-like editorial layouts.** Think The Economist, Stripe documentation, IBM Research. Content-first, restrained, substantive.
- **A restrained palette.** Light and dark neutrals as the base, purple as the accent that leads the eye, Blu and Violet only where a design needs to stand out. Never neon, never "Web3 gradient overload". See [Colors and typography](colors-typography.md).
- **Quality typography with clear hierarchy.** One typeface, Figtree. Hierarchy comes from weight and size: Semibold for titles and display numbers, Regular for text and data numbers. Two weights only.
- **Generous whitespace.** Space around content signals confidence. Clutter signals anxiety.
- **Data visualized beautifully.** Charts, patents map, node distribution. Evidence presented with care. Every chart and diagram carries a caption stating what it shows. Every number carries its source and date.

## Avoid

- **Generic "futuristic" 3D renders.** Glowing cubes, hexagonal grids, matrix-style code. Visual cliché of everyone else.
- **Stock photos of abstract "tech".** Close-ups of circuit boards, anonymous hands on keyboards. Absence of humanity.
- **Aggressive gradients, glowing effects, "cyberpunk" aesthetics.** These belong to the crypto-project visual culture we're outgrowing.
- **Meme-style graphics, cartoons, emoji-heavy design.** Belongs to the Jester archetype.
- **Fake diversity stock photography.** Caregiver values authenticity. Use real people wherever possible.

## The mascot

The astronaut belongs to the Explorer and Jester archetypes, not to Sage-Caregiver.

| Allowed | Never |
|---|---|
| Community channels, merchandise, ecosystem content, Cardano-native moments, Web3 and Cardano builders, individual and retail users | Enterprise, banking, healthcare, investor, regulatory materials |
