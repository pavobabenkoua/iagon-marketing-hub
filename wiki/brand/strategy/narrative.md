## Brand Narrative · The story behind Iagon

The canonical story. The short version below is derived from the core version and must not contradict it.

### Core version (~300 words)

In 2017, four PhDs asked a question that most cloud companies never get around to: who actually owns the data that defines your life?

The question came from healthcare. Medical records, the most intimate data a person generates, were stored on infrastructure patients didn't control, couldn't audit, and often couldn't access. The data was about the patient, but it was never theirs. The founders of Iagon (a medical entrepreneur, an ex-Huawei CTO with 12 patents, an ex-Expedia and ex-Google engineering leader, and an AI economist) recognized this wasn't a healthcare problem. It was the defining problem of the cloud era.

So they began building the infrastructure that could fix it.

Since then, Iagon has become the patented architecture behind the Shared Storage and Compute Economy: a global, decentralized network where data ownership, compliance, and control belong to the people who create the data, not the platforms that store it. Our technology is protected in 15+ jurisdictions, security-reviewed and audited across key components, and lives on Cardano, a blockchain built for systems that must operate for decades.

Today, our focus is enterprise: the organizations with the strictest requirements for their data. Fireblocks, which serves 2400+ institutions, connects its clients to Cardano staking and governance through the Iagon Insight API. Würth validates 3D printing IP licensing on our network. Volksbank builds user-owned data vaults on our architecture.

Designed for Enterprise. Built for Everyone. Because the right to own your data doesn't belong to the few. It belongs to anyone who has data worth protecting.

Your data, your control, your future.

### Short version (~60 words)

The answer became Iagon, the patented architecture behind the Shared Storage and Compute Economy, where data ownership, compliance, and control belong to the people who create the data, not the platforms that store it. Designed for Enterprise. Built for Everyone.

### Where each version goes

One story, two lengths. The core version is the source of truth. The short version is derived from it and must never contradict it.

| Version | Where |
|---|---|
| Short, ~60 words | Social bios, website hero subtext, email signatures, conference programmes, and quick introductions at partner meetings. Anywhere the reader gives us fifteen seconds. |
| Core, ~300 words | About page, press kit, investor one-pager, partnership briefs, and spokesperson preparation before interviews. This is the version journalists quote, so it should be easy to copy and easy to lift a paragraph from. |

> **Rule:** Short is not a trimmed Core, and Core is not a padded Short. Each version is written for its own surface and its own reader. What stays identical across both is the opening question, the healthcare origin, the category name, and the closing line.
>
> **Longer formats.** Founder's letters, annual recaps, careers pages, and keynote scripts are built from the Core version when the moment calls for them. They expand the same story, they never replace it. Anyone writing one starts from the Core version and adds detail, not a different narrative.
