## Vision · The world we're building toward

**A world built on self-sovereign data, where every person, business, and institution controls what's theirs, by design.**

Our Vision is not a description of our product. It's a description of the world our product makes possible. Our health, our finances, our work, our identity, our institutions. All of it is data now. We believe that is a historical accident, not a permanent condition. Self-sovereign data is the property a world has when its people, companies, and institutions can control what's theirs: not by permission, not by process, but by the architecture of the systems they rely on. That is the world Iagon exists to build.

> ℹ️ Self-sovereign data is the state of the world. Data sovereignty is the property of a system that produces it. Iagon builds the second to make the first possible.

## Mission · How we get there

**We're building the Shared Storage and Compute Economy: a global, decentralized infrastructure layer where data ownership, compliance, and control belong to the people who create the data, not the platforms that store it.**

Our mission names the category we're creating: the Shared Storage and Compute Economy. It's an economy because it turns the world's idle digital resources into shared infrastructure. A large share of global storage and compute capacity sits unused at any moment. That capacity already exists, already consumes power, and already sits in data centres and offices around the world. We turn it into infrastructure someone needs. It's decentralized because no single entity should control the data of billions. And it's compliant by design because privacy, sovereignty, and regulation are not features we add. They're the foundation we build on.

### How to use Vision and Mission

**Do**
- Use the Vision when speaking to the world: keynotes, manifestos, campaign headlines, investor decks, op-eds, community moments.
- Use the Mission when speaking to buyers and partners: sales decks, product pages, partnership briefs, enterprise RFPs.
- Always pair the two when introducing Iagon for the first time. Vision without Mission sounds abstract. Mission without Vision sounds like just another vendor.
- When there is room for only one, use the Mission. It carries the category name, and the category is what we need the market to repeat.

**Don't**
- Don't shorten the Vision to fit a tagline. It is a strategic statement, not a line for a headline. The lines built for headlines are the [DNA Anchors](dna-anchors.md).
- Don't swap "people, businesses, institutions" for a single audience. The universality is the point: Iagon serves all three.
- Don't describe Iagon as "a company that offers cloud storage" in official communication. We're the infrastructure layer for the Shared Storage and Compute Economy.
