## The Compliance Gap · The problem we exist to close

Regulation is clear about what it wants. Sensitive data stays under the owner's control, inside agreed jurisdictions, with every access accounted for. The infrastructure most organizations rent cannot do any of that structurally.

> So the gap gets filled by other means. Process. Paperwork. Certifications. Contractual assurances. Audits that describe what should happen rather than enforce it. The work is real, the cost is permanent, and the gap never fully closes, because the architecture underneath it never changed.
>
> We call this the Compliance Gap. It is the problem our category exists to solve, and it is the first thing we explain in any serious conversation.

Iagon closes the gap in the architecture. Compliance conditions are set by the customer and enforced by the network before data is placed anywhere. That is the difference between compliance by process and compliance by design.
