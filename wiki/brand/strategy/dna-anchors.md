## DNA Anchors · What we say

Three phrases that carry the load of everything Iagon communicates. They earn a place in headlines, keynotes, pitch closings, and investor decks. Every piece of external language should ladder up to at least one of them.

| | Emotional Anchor | Rational Anchor | Strategic Anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Line** | Your data, your control, your future. | Compliance by design, not by process. | Designed for Enterprise, Built for Everyone. |
| **Use** | The line we leave people with. Use at the end of manifestos, campaigns, keynotes. | The line that wins enterprise trust. Use in sales decks, product pages, regulatory briefs. | The line that names our market position. Use in partnership briefs, presentations, category-defining moments. |
| **Why it's true** | Sovereignty first. We give users control even when it is harder to build. See [Core Values](core-values.md). | Compliance conditions are enforced by the network before data is placed. See [The Compliance Gap](compliance-gap.md). | We build for the customers with the strictest requirements. See [the Lighthouse Principle](positioning.md#the-lighthouse-principle). |

DNA anchors are what we say. Core values are how we earn the right to say it.
