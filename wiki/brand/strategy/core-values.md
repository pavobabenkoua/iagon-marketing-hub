## Core Values · How we work

Five principles that govern how we make decisions, build products, hire, and serve customers. Every value is tied to the DNA anchor it proves. If a decision doesn't honor these, we don't make it.

### Sovereignty first
*Proves the emotional anchor.*

We default to giving users control, even when it's harder to build. Sovereignty is not a feature we add. It's the constraint we design against from day one.

- **In practice:** when engineering must choose between a faster centralized shortcut and a slower sovereign path, we take the sovereign path.
- **We reject:** dark patterns, opaque terms of service, convenience-for-control trade-offs, and lock-in architectures.

### Build it right, not fast
*Proves the rational anchor.*

We'd rather ship later than ship compromised. In infrastructure, shortcuts become tomorrow's breaches. Our customers' data is mission-critical, and our standard must match the stakes.

- **In practice:** security audits, patent protection, peer-reviewed architecture, before launch, not after. No "ship now, harden later."
- **We reject:** the move-fast-and-break-things philosophy. That belongs to social apps, not banking infrastructure.

### Earn the hard customer
*Proves the strategic anchor.*

We optimize for the customer with the highest standards: banks, healthcare, regulated enterprise. If we can serve them, we can serve anyone. This is why we're Designed for Enterprise, Built for Everyone.

- **In practice:** the product roadmap is driven by Fortune 500 and regulated industry requirements first. Consumer ease comes from clearing the higher bar.
- **We reject:** lowest-common-denominator design. Features that would never pass a compliance review.

### Verifiable by design
*Proves the rational anchor.*

Trust in infrastructure is not claimed. It's demonstrated. We publish what proves the system works: patents, audits, on-chain economics, governance, and architectural reviews. What we protect, we protect openly, and explain why.

- **What we publish:** patent filings in 15+ jurisdictions. Independent security audits. On-chain treasury and token flows. Governance decisions. Partnership structures, architecture research. All network figures are maintained in the Iagon Ecosystem Stats App and published with a date. See [A note on figures](positioning.md#a-note-on-figures).
- **What we protect:** the patented core architecture, legally, to protect the ecosystem from replication. Enterprise deployments, contractually, to honor our customers' compliance and confidentiality requirements.
- **We reject:** marketing claims we can't back with evidence, audits, or code. "Trust us" as an answer. Hidden governance. Undisclosed economic interests.

> Iagon is not open-source by default. It is verifiable by default. These are different things, and the distinction matters. Open-source assumes code visibility equals trust. Verifiable means we provide the evidence that lets any stakeholder (regulator, customer, investor, community) independently confirm we do what we say. Patents make our core defensible. Audits make it credible. On-chain economics make it auditable. Together, they build a trust model stronger than open-source alone.

### Own the outcome
*Proves the rational anchor.*

Every team member is accountable for what the customer experiences, not just their slice of the stack. Decentralized architecture demands distributed ownership of results.

- **In practice:** if a partner's pilot fails, everyone involved owns that failure. If compliance breaks, it's not "legal's problem". It's the company's.
- **We reject:** silo thinking. "Not my team" deflections. Handoffs without follow-through.

### How to use this system

| For | How |
|---|---|
| Marketers | Every piece of external content should visibly ladder up to at least one DNA anchor. If you can't name the anchor a headline serves, rewrite it. |
| Product | Feature proposals must pass at least one core value test. If a feature violates "Sovereignty first", it doesn't ship, regardless of demand. |
| Hiring | Interview for values alignment, not just skills. Candidates who don't understand "Build it right, not fast" won't survive the culture. |
| Sales | Lead enterprise conversations with the rational DNA anchor. Lead community conversations with the emotional DNA anchor. Both sides honor the same values. |
| Leadership | When two values conflict in a decision, document the trade-off openly. Values tension is the sign of a real system, not a broken one. |
