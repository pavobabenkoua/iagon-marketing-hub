## Brand Archetype · How Iagon plays in the minds of its audiences

The psychological role Iagon plays in the minds of its audiences. This is the single source of truth for every creative decision: voice, visuals, content, hiring, storytelling.

The 12 classical archetypes: Hero, Outlaw, Magician, Explorer, Lover, Jester, Innocent, Everyman, Creator, Caregiver, Sage, Ruler.

**Iagon is the Sage with the soul of the Caregiver.**

Iagon speaks like a Sage and acts like a Caregiver. We earn authority through rigor, evidence, and intellectual seriousness, the way a scientist, a trusted doctor, or a top-tier research institution earns it. But the reason we do any of it is protective: we stand between people, businesses, and institutions and the loss of control over what's theirs. Sage is our voice. Caregiver is our motive. The combination is rare, and it is what makes Iagon possible.

| | The Sage: how we speak, present, and prove | The Caregiver: why we build and for whom |
|---|---|---|
| **Belief** | The world improves through understanding. Sages are trusted because they demonstrate, not declare. Rigor over volume. Evidence over claim. | Exists to protect what matters. Stands between their people and risk. Protection by building, not posturing. |
| **Desire** | Truth, understanding, proof | To protect, to serve, to defend |
| **Fear** | Being wrong, being deceived, ignorance | Failure to protect, harm done to those in their care |
| **Strategy** | Research, analyze, publish, teach | Build defenses, create safety, anticipate risk |
| **Gift** | Wisdom, perspective, credibility | Safety, trust, sovereignty |

### Voice dimensions

Each dimension of voice is tuned to the Sage–Caregiver combination. Sage calibrates the register; Caregiver calibrates the temperature.

| Dimension | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Register | Intellectually serious, not academic | We speak with expertise but never condescend. A researcher explaining to a colleague, not a professor to a student. |
| Tone | Warm precision | Caregiver brings humanity to Sage's rigor. "We built this because we know what happens when you can't access your own medical record," not "Feature enabled for HIPAA compliance." |
| Energy | Calm and steady | Sage-Caregiver brands do not raise their voice. Authority is earned through composure. No hype, no urgency theatre. |
| Vocabulary | Precise, evidence-led, human | Prefer specific over abstract. "A network spanning 50+ countries" over "global network." "Healthcare records" over "sensitive data." |
| Humor | Dry, restrained, rare | Sage humor works. Jester humor does not. If in doubt, leave it out. |
| Authority | Show, don't assert | "15+ patent jurisdictions" earns authority. "We are the leader in…" does not. |

### Voice in practice: examples

- **Sage-Caregiver voice (correct):** *"Medical records are the most intimate data a person generates. They sat on infrastructure patients didn't control and often couldn't access. We built Iagon to change that, for them, and for everyone whose data is owned by someone else."*
- **Pure Sage (too cold, Caregiver missing):** *"Iagon provides a patented decentralized architecture for healthcare data management, meeting HIPAA and GDPR compliance standards across 15+ jurisdictions."*
- **Pure Caregiver (too soft, Sage missing):** *"Your medical data is precious. We care about keeping it safe for you. With Iagon, you and your family can feel confident your information is in good hands."*

### Content implications

| Content we make | Content we avoid |
|---|---|
| **Research papers and whitepapers.** Long-form, evidence-led, technical where needed. | **Hype posts, hot-takes, reaction content.** Sage-Caregiver does not chase timelines. |
| **Case studies with named outcomes.** How Würth validates 3D printing IP licensing. What Volksbank's data vaults achieve. | **Meme marketing, crypto-Twitter banter.** We are infrastructure, not entertainment. |
| **Explainers that teach.** "What self-sovereign data actually means." "Why compliance-by-design is different." | **Celebrity endorsements, influencer campaigns.** Authority comes from evidence, not fame. |
| **Human stories behind infrastructure.** Why a patient in 2017 couldn't access their records. The people the technology protects. | **"Disruption" and "revolution" language.** Rulers disrupt. Sages explain. Caregivers protect. |
| **Peer-reviewed architectural content.** Audit reports, protocol deep-dives, patent explanations. | **Urgency marketing, FOMO, scarcity plays.** The Sage has no need to rush; the Caregiver never manipulates. |
| **Testimony-style content.** Interviews with CTOs, compliance officers, researchers, not influencers. | **Generic "thought leadership" on trending topics.** We write about what we know, deeply. Not what's in the news, thinly. |

### Visual implications

Visuals flow directly from the archetype. Sage visuals signal credibility and depth; Caregiver visuals signal humanity and warmth. Iagon's visuals carry both.

The visual rules that follow from the archetype live in the design system: [Art direction](../visual/art-direction.md).

### Storytelling implications

Every brand tells the same handful of stories over and over.

| Stories we tell | Stories we avoid |
|---|---|
| **The hidden problem revealed.** "What we discovered about [data ownership in X industry]." Sage frame: revealing truth. | **The rebellion against the establishment.** Outlaw archetype. We replace the system, we don't fight it. |
| **The person behind the infrastructure.** "A patient. A surgeon. A bank customer. A 3D printing engineer." Caregiver frame: who we protect. | **The magical transformation story.** Magician archetype. We don't transform people, we protect what's already theirs. |
| **The proof in the work.** "How Würth validates 3D printing IP with Iagon." "What our audit found." Sage frame: evidence. | **The heroic founder story.** Hero archetype. Our founders are researchers and engineers, not champions. We tell the story of the problem and the people it affects, not the story of the people who solved it. |
| **The principle held.** "Why we chose a harder path." "What we refused to compromise on." Caregiver frame: integrity. | |

### Inspiration and divergence

| Learn from | Diverge from |
|---|---|
| **Stripe.** Sage at its best. Intellectual rigor, beautiful documentation, restraint. No hype, no influencers, just precision and proof. Iagon's content should aspire to this standard. | **Filecoin / Storj marketing.** Generic Web3 Explorer voice: "frontiers," "new worlds," crypto-native aesthetics. Iagon is not an explorer; we are a Sage-Caregiver infrastructure company. |
| **Cloudflare.** Sage with a mission. Technical authority and a strong stance on an open internet. Shows how infrastructure brands can hold values without becoming activists. | **Oracle.** Pure Ruler. Cold, institutional, authority-by-decree. We want enterprise credibility without the distance. |
| **Volvo.** Caregiver at the enterprise level. Safety as brand DNA, proven through engineering. Shows how protection can be premium, not patronizing. | **Consumer crypto brands (Coinbase, etc.).** Hero-Everyman voice. Democratization narrative, approachability at the cost of rigor. We are more serious than that. |
| **The Economist.** Sage voice on editorial. Calm, intelligent, evidence-first. Tone model for Iagon's longform content. | **AWS / Azure / GCP.** Generic enterprise tech voice. Features-first, humanity-absent. Iagon's Caregiver soul is our differentiator; don't mute it. |

> **The one-sentence archetype test**
>
> Before publishing any piece of content, running any campaign, approving any visual, or writing any copy, ask: *"Does this sound like a trusted expert who cares about the people on the other side?"*
>
> If yes, it's Sage-Caregiver. If it sounds like a salesman, it's wrong. If it sounds like an academic, add warmth. If it sounds like a cheerleader, add rigor. This single test replaces a hundred brand guidelines.
