## Formats and limits
- **Long posts.** The account has X Premium: a post can run to 25,000 characters. The feed shows about the first 280, then "Show more". Put what matters in the first 280.
- **Threads.** For content split into independent parts (by product, by area), a thread beats one long post: every tweet is its own entry point that the right audience can repost.
- **Single image:** 1200×700 ([canvas sizes](../brand/visual/canvases.md)).
- **Several images in one post:** X shows them as a carousel and crops each image from the centre to about 7:8. Use 1080×1080 with a 112 px side safe zone, as in [monthly recap cards](../brand/visual/formats/monthly-recap-cards.md). Check the crop in the composer before posting.
- **Text markup:** none. `**bold**` shows as asterisks.
