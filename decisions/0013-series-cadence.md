# 0013: Cadence separates series

## Context
[0009](0009-series-registry.md) allows a new series only if no existing series covers the same narrative and audience. Development Update Weekly and the Monthly Recap share both, yet they have different jobs, channels and rhythm. One series can't hold two cadences: the registry measures overdue by cadence.

## Decision
A new series is justified when it differs from every existing series in narrative, audience **or cadence**.

## Consequences
Development Update Weekly is its own series, next to the Monthly Recap.
